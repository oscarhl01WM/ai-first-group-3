# B-M4-01: Verify price source, coverage, and freshness readiness for pilot

**Status:** Draft for review and assignment
**Owner:** B — Backend / Prices and Supermarkets
**Milestone / priority:** M4 — pilot and release decision
**Related requirements:** FR-08–FR-10
**Collaborators:** A coordinates pilot communication; C reviews ingredient mappings; D coordinates readiness and release evidence.

## Description
Before and during pilot, verify that each data source/branch remains within approved rights and scope, mappings are reviewed, observation ages meet the team-adopted policy, and known gaps are clearly reported. Provide evidence to the joint release decision.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Current source permissions and access evidence are reviewed and recorded for every pilot source.
- [ ] Coverage, mapping review state, and observation freshness are reported against team-approved criteria.
- [ ] Expired promotions, missing prices, and blocked sources are visible as limitations.
- [ ] The team does not claim FR-08 complete without permitted scraping or an approved scope change.

## Dependencies
- B-M0-01/M0-02 approved source and freshness decisions; B-M2-01/M2-02 operational data; D-M4-01 release readiness.

## Open questions and constraints
- Pilot coverage targets must be explicitly adopted; proposed measures are not default gates.
